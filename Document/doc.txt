# The Solar System

## The Sun

The Sun is the star at the center of the Solar System. It contains about 99.86% of the Solar System's total mass. Its gravity keeps planets and other objects in orbit. The Sun's core reaches about 15 million°C.

## Inner Planets

The four inner planets are Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars. They are terrestrial planets made mainly of rock and metal.

**Mercury** is the smallest planet and closest to the Sun. It has almost no atmosphere and experiences extreme temperature changes.

**Venus** is the hottest planet. Its thick carbon-dioxide atmosphere creates a strong greenhouse effect that traps heat.

**Earth** is the third planet from the Sun and the only planet currently known to support life. It has abundant liquid water.

**Mars** is called the Red Planet because of iron oxide on its surface. It contains Olympus Mons, the largest known volcano in the Solar System.

## Outer Planets

The four outer planets are Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune.

**Jupiter** is the largest planet and a gas giant. It is famous for the Great Red Spot, a massive storm.

**Saturn** is a gas giant famous for its large ring system made mainly of ice and rocky particles.

**Uranus** is an ice giant with a blue-green color caused partly by methane. Its axis is tilted about 98°, so it rotates almost on its side.

**Neptune** is the eighth and most distant planet. It is an ice giant known for extremely strong winds.

## Other Objects

The **asteroid belt** lies mainly between Mars and Jupiter and contains many rocky objects.

**Pluto** is a dwarf planet. It was reclassified from a planet to a dwarf planet in 2006.

The Solar System formed approximately **4.6 billion years ago**.
