# HR Policy — Nexora Technologies

**Document ID:** HR-001
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026

> This is a fictional document created for testing purposes.

## 1. Working Hours

Employees work from **9:30 AM to 6:30 PM**, Monday to Friday. Lunch break is from **1:00 PM to 2:00 PM**.

## 2. Leave Policy

Employees receive:

* Casual Leave: **8 days/year**
* Sick Leave: **8 days/year**
* Earned Leave: **15 days/year**

Earned leave should normally be requested at least **7 days in advance**.

## 3. Work From Home

Eligible employees can request up to **8 WFH days per month** with manager approval.

## 4. Probation

New employees have a standard **6-month probation period**.

## 5. Performance Review

Performance reviews are conducted **twice a year**, normally in **June and December**.

## 6. Resignation

The standard notice period is **60 calendar days**, unless the employment agreement specifies otherwise.

## 7. Workplace Conduct

Employees must maintain professional behavior. Harassment, discrimination, bullying, and unauthorized disclosure of confidential information are prohibited.

## 8. Confidentiality

Employees must protect company information, including customer data, source code, financial information, and internal documents.

**End of Policy**
